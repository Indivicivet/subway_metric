Data and estimates

Times represent typical weekday daytime travel. Walking uses mapped streets and paths at 4.8 km/h, with a slower pace on stairs. Train waits use half the representative daytime headway. Station access and transfers include estimated walking inside stations.

Guangzhou Metro

Ordered metro stops, track alignments, station exits, and walking paths come from the OpenStreetMap network and the Geofabrik Guangdong extract. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License 1.0. Download the Guangzhou routing database and map metadata.

Train times are estimates from track distance, assumed line speeds, and a stop allowance. Waits and transfers use assumed times. Where a mapped entrance cannot be connected to a walking path, routing uses the station centre.

Other mainland metro systems

Stops, tracks, entrances and walking paths for Zhengzhou, Shanghai, Chongqing, Chengdu, Beijing, Qingdao and Shenzhen are from Geofabrik's regional OpenStreetMap extracts, © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License 1.0. Chinese station names are shown where mapped, alongside English names where available.

Train running times are estimates based on track distance at an assumed 52 km/h plus 20 seconds per segment. Dwell is assumed to be 25 seconds, headways six minutes, and transfers two minutes plus estimated walking time. Express services are not included. Mapped entrances are matched to nearby stations; station centres are used where no entrance connects to a walking path.

Coverage follows mapped service relations and may be incomplete. Beijing Line 18 uses the operator's station order with OpenStreetMap coordinates for stations omitted by its route relations. Chengdu Line 30 uses the published station order and excludes the two stations listed as unopened in the opening report. Unlinked stations appear in each city's map metadata. Shanghai requires both the Shanghai and Jiangsu extracts to cover Line 11's Huaqiao branch.

In the current extracts, Chengdu Tianfu Railway Station (天府站) and Qingdao Guzhenkou (古镇口) have no usable walking connection in the bundle. Routes can pass through them but cannot start or end there via the walking graph.
